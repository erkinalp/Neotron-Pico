## Key Points for Expansion Slots and Connectors

1. **Expansion Slot Orientation**: Maintained original orientation (no mirroring)
2. **Expansion Slot Position**: Positioned according to microBTX specifications
3. **Expansion Slot Clearance**: Ensured proper clearance for card insertion and removal
4. **External Connector Position**: Positioned along the edge of the board
5. **External Connector Orientation**: Ensured proper orientation for compatibility with external devices
6. **External Connector Clearance**: Ensured proper clearance for external access
